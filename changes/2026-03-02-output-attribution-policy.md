# Output attribution policy

- **Date:** 2026-03-02
- **Branch:** `chore/ban-output-attribution`
- **Target:** `on-going-dev`

## What changed

- Strengthened the repository policy against automated tooling attribution,
  branding, credits, badges, trailers, and links.
- Extended the prohibition to every publishing surface, including PR comments
  and reviews, and required outgoing-text inspection before publication.

## Why

Keep project output free of unrelated tooling promotion or attribution.

## Migration or breaking notes

None.
